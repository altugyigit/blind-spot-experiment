Blind-Spot Experiment

Keep your phone horizontal.
Close your right eye.
Keep your left eye focused on the +.
Do not look directly at the black dot.
+
Slowly move the phone toward or away from your face.

Keep looking only at the +.
At a certain distance, the black dot should disappear!
